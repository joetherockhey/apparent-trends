Things you should definitely ghost

Creators dress as ghosts holding signboards that list common mistakes or bad habits in their industry, ending with a better solution.

FormatSkit with signboards in ghost costume

Why nowIt is the peak of Halloween season and combines seasonal fun with educational authority.

How a brand could use it

A finance client could have a 'ghost' list outdated saving habits to avoid, while a tech client could list 'ghosting' old software for their new platform. It’s a low-cost way to show personality while providing value.

A banking brand uses a ghost to list 'hidden fees' and 'manual transfers' as things to ghost in favor of their app.

Getting robbed

A single-shot comedy skit where the creator is so distracted by their phone that they don't notice someone stealing their bag in the background.

FormatOne-shot video with text overlay

Why nowIt is a high-engagement humor format that peaked in mid-October.

How a brand could use it

An entertainment or news client can use this to show how 'addictive' or 'engaging' their content is. The text overlay would say 'Me when [Brand Name] drops a new episode/article.'

A streaming service shows a viewer losing their bag because they are too focused on a season finale.

Watch outEnsure the 'theft' is clearly comedic and staged to avoid alarming viewers.

The 'Oh look' trend

A duo reacts with dramatic panic to a stressful scenario before instantly switching to calm when a simple solution is presented.

FormatShort skit video

Soundsonido original - user077983456

Why nowIt is a highly effective 'problem/solution' framework currently circulating.

How a brand could use it

Ideal for B2B or service-based clients. Show the 'panic' of a complex task (like tax or logistics) and the 'calm' of using the client's service.

A logistics brand shows a business owner panicking over a lost shipment, then instantly calm when they open the brand's tracking app.

How this works

Each Monday, trend round-ups from New Engen, Later, SocialPilot, Gelato, Ramdam and others are read and merged into one list. Anything stale or hard to use for a brand is dropped. Example posts link to the original creators, and screenshots are there for reference only.

The descriptions and brand ideas are a starting point. Check a trend's sound and licensing before using it on a brand account.
