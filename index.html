Preparing for my Netflix documentary

Creator mimes clipping on a lapel mic and sits in an interview chair while on-screen text frames a petty or awkward life event as their Netflix documentary.

Usage
438.7K TikTok videos on the sound (20 Oct 2026); top post 35.7M views, 5.7M likes. Anijam says it crossed to Instagram in Oct 2026 (no reel count).
Origin
Started by TikTok creator @borderbandittx on 16 Jun 2026; posts by @fombasyllah and @wassupa1ex on 30 Jun took it viral.
Sound
On a Mission, Duomo
Brand idea
Frame a small brand drama as a documentary premise, e.g. 'Preparing for my Netflix documentary about the day we ran out of croissants by 7am.'
Watch out
Avoid Netflix logos or the Netflix sting; keep the parody generic.
  • @suckafreekells35.7M plays · 5.7M likes · top post, 2 Jul 2026
  • @borderbandittx3.9M plays · 420K likes · origin, 16 Jun 2026
  • @fombasyllah21.7M plays · 3.6M likes · early viral post, 30 Jun 2026

Money can't buy happiness, but...

On-screen text reads 'Money can't buy happiness, but it can buy...' over a short clip of the thing that obviously makes you happy.

Usage

Sound

Brand idea
A product shot with a punchline: 'but it can buy' then the hero product or a customer's reaction. Works for retail, food, beauty and experiences.
Watch out

03Past peakWorks any weekTikTok

I just want to be OK

Creators dance or act cheerful to the 'I just want to be OK' chorus while the caption reveals the mess or stress they are ignoring.

Usage
204.5K TikTok videos on the sound (20 Oct 2026, includes pre-2026 use); top post 18.5M views, 5M likes
Origin
A 2008 Ingrid Michaelson song, revived on TikTok around 20 to 22 Aug 2026; the earliest big posts found are @madozilla and @camilla.
Sound
Be OK, Ingrid Michaelson
Brand idea
Staff dance happily while the caption names a customer headache (tax time, hail claims, a full inbox), then cut to the product sorting it.
Watch out
Some creators use it for sincere mental-health posts, so keep it light and never mock real distress.

  • @haileigh.freeman28.9M plays · 2.4M likes · most-viewed post found, 30 Aug 2026

Pumpkin Head trend

Friends, couples or families wear hollowed carved pumpkins over their heads and stage an everyday scene or photoshoot, set to 'Oh Klahoma'.

Usage
